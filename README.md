If you're staying in Sharm El Sheikh and looking for an exciting day away from the resort, a trip to Ras Mohammed National Park and Magic Lake is a fantastic choice.

## 🐠 Discover Ras Mohammed National Park

Ras Mohammed is one of the most beautiful natural destinations in South Sinai. The area is famous for its crystal-clear Red Sea waters, colorful coral reefs, and fascinating marine life.

Enjoy snorkeling in the clear waters and discover the underwater beauty of the Red Sea.

## 🌴 Explore the Mangrove Channel

The Mangrove Channel is another fascinating stop during the adventure. Enjoy the peaceful surroundings and discover the unique mangrove environment.

## 🏜️ Visit the Gate of Allah

The famous Gate of Allah is a great place to stop for photos and enjoy the spectacular desert scenery of Sinai.

## 🌊 Swim at Magic Lake

One of the highlights of the trip is Magic Lake. Surrounded by beautiful desert landscapes, it is a wonderful place to swim, relax, and enjoy the natural beauty of Sinai.

## 📸 Discover the Earthquake Crack

The Earthquake Crack offers another fascinating look at the geological history of the area and makes an interesting stop during the day.
